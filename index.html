About Fight On Founders

As part of the Iovine and Young Academy's Innovation Quest, Fight On Founders brings USC students together to explore problem spaces, learn from experts, and build real startup skills.

Innovation Quest

A comprehensive program designed to foster entrepreneurial thinking and hands-on startup experience within the USC community.

Iovine and Young Academy

USC's premier program combining technology, business, and design to create the next generation of innovative leaders.

Students work on actual problems, developing solutions that have the potential to make a real difference in the world.
